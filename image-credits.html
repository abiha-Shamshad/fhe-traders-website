Image Credits

Where the photography and video on this site comes from, and how it is licensed.

Site photography

The lifestyle and installation photography across this site — the home page, services, gallery, about and blog pages — comes from Pexels.

The Pexels License permits free use for commercial purposes and does not require attribution. This page is published as a courtesy record of sourcing, not because attribution is required.

Home page video

The looping video behind the home page headline is not stock footage. It was composed from five of the still photographs above, with a slow camera move applied to each and cross-fades between them. It carries the same license as the source images.

Product photography

Product images in the catalog are manufacturer and distributor photographs of the goods we stock, used to identify those products. Brands and product names are the property of their respective owners.

Where we could not source a genuine image of a product, the catalog shows a category icon rather than a substitute photograph — we would rather show no photo than the wrong one.

Replacing these with your own

Photographs of your own completed jobs will always outperform stock imagery. Any file in images/site/ can be swapped for one of your own with the same filename, and it will appear across the site with no other change needed.

Spotted an issue with an image?

If any image on this site is used in error, message us and we will remove it promptly.
