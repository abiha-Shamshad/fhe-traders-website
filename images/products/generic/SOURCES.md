# Representative product photos — sourcing record

The files in this folder are **representative photos of a product type**, not
photographs of the specific brand's own item. They are used for cards whose
brand publishes no product photography anywhere online: Mekaal, Zec, Al Falah,
Al Marth, the FHE house brand, and several Evoo lines.

Every card carrying one of these has `photoIsRepresentative: true` in
`js/products-data.js`, which makes `product.html` print a note telling the
visitor the picture is a stand-in. `image-credits.html` says the same thing.

**Licensing:** almost all of these are marketplace listing photographs (Amazon
product images, AliExpress listing images, two Pakistani retailers). Free-licence
libraries were tried first and were unusable from this network — Wikimedia
Commons does not resolve, Pexels/Pixabay/Unsplash need API keys, and Openverse
results for these product types were not usable product shots.

Marketplace photos are a **weaker licensing position** than a manufacturer photo
of goods you actually sell. Replacing these with your own shelf photographs is
the durable fix: drop a file in with the same name and nothing else changes.
